In build, not live · tested with 8 people.

  • Problem. About 5,000 trade-reporting breaks a day for US FX alone, each with seven business days to be fixed and proven. Operations worked them by hand in a spreadsheet with macros and in Jira. The Jira licence was ending in September and an audit item was due in December.
  • What I did. Joined as the only designer and took over a prototype built around the bank's systems. I agreed a quality bar with operations and engineering before redesigning anything, then rebuilt the prototype around the operator's day: breaks grouped by the field that is wrong, and a second person signing off every fix. AI kept the records. People made every decision.
  • Result. The same eight people who rated the old way 3.2 out of 5 on ExQual rated the rebuild 4.6, in the Platinum band, against a Bronze target. Product and engineering got an agreed, build-ready scope in about two months. It is in build, not live.
