In build, not live · tested with 8 people

  • Problem. An operations team at NAB finds and fixes faulty trade reports, called breaks, and has seven business days to fix and prove each one. For US FX alone there are about 5,000 a day, and the team worked them by hand in a spreadsheet with macros and in Jira. The Jira licence was ending in September and an audit item was due in December.
  • What I did. I joined as the only designer on RegShield, a review tool for regulatory reporting, and took over a prototype built around the bank's systems. Before redesigning anything, I agreed a quality bar with operations and engineering. The redesign follows the operator's day, with breaks grouped by the field that is wrong and a second person signing off every fix. I used AI to keep the project records, and every decision in them was made by a person.
  • Result. The same eight people rated the new design 4.6 out of 5 on ExQual, the bank's experience score, up from 3.2 for the old way of working. That puts it in the Platinum band, above the Bronze target. Product and engineering had an agreed scope ready to build in about two months. It is in build and not yet live, and the scores come from testing a prototype.
