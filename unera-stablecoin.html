Five people scored it 83.5 out of 100 for ease of use, against a target of 70.

In-flight product · design complete, with engineering

  • Problem. People want to see what backs a stablecoin, a digital dollar, before they trust it. Early designs for Unera's portal leaned on claims such as “fully backed” and “instant redemption”. Some were not strictly true, and none could be checked on screen.
  • What I did. Designed the web portal where people buy, hold and redeem hUSD, a US-dollar stablecoin. After a back-end engineer's comment, took every claim we couldn't prove off the screen, and redesigned Proof of Reserve so each number shows its date and its source. Recommended cutting the first release to four jobs, gave each slow or failed path its own state, and kept motion on the public pages to pieces that show money going somewhere.
  • Result. The design is complete and handed to engineering. It has not launched yet. Three claims came off the screen, nine jobs became four, and 259 functional and 95 responsive test cases were written next to the screens, so QA tests what was intended. At the end of my design work, five people tested the prototype and scored it 83.5 out of 100 for ease of use (SUS), against a target of 70.
