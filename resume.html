Summary

Ten years in product design, after four as a front-end developer. I design products people have to trust with their money, data or security, and I lead by writing the standard down and coaching people against it. Two of my releases are measured in production; the rest of my numbers say how they were measured.

Experience

  1. Senior Product Designer · National Australia Bank, Ho Chi Minh City

    Feb 2024 – now
    • Corporate & Institutional Banking (May 2025 – now). UX owner for credit submissions. Led the design of straight-through processing (Jan–Jun 2026, live since mid-2026), which moved the hand-off of approved facilities from email into the credit platform. In testing with 6 bankers it took about 5 minutes instead of up to a day; ExQual 4.40, Gold against a Silver target.
    • Redesigned the single large exposure check: 67% fewer clicks to review and 39% fewer to simulate a change, ExQual 4.57 / 5 in five moderated sessions against a Silver target.
    • Sole designer on RegShield, an AI-assisted tool for trade-reporting breaks across four regulators. Rebuilt an inherited prototype around the operator’s day: the same 8 people scored the old tools 3.2 and the rebuild 4.6 on ExQual, Platinum against a Bronze target. In build, not live. 23 decisions logged from 27 working sessions.
    • Merchant Services (Feb 2024 – Apr 2025). Led the onboarding redesign across two channels and three products. Time to apply fell from 21–25 minutes to about 10 in testing (n=10), SUS 80.7, and dropout fell by a relative 20% after Phase 1. Mentored two designers.
    • AI adoption lead for the Vietnam design chapter since March 2026. Q2 FY26 individual award; Q3 FY26 individual award for straight-through processing. Winner, Design Day Challenge 3.
