Six people scored it 81 out of 100 for ease of use, against a target of 70.

In-flight product · design complete, with engineering

  • Problem. Donors often have no proof of where their money goes, and on a blockchain a normal wait can look like a failure. The client wanted one wallet for giving, sending and earning points, for people with no crypto knowledge, on phone and desktop.
  • What I did. Designed the consumer app across 43 pages and built the design system behind it. Made one tracker for every money flow, and redesigned the flows when the team agreed to move the wallet and identity gates to the end. With no process and feedback from many places, I set up a feedback workshop every two weeks, and the design followed what we agreed in the spec. I designed the happy path and a sample of the error and edge cases with QA, and a junior designer completed the rest to written rules. I joined for the interface and was later promoted to lead.
  • Result. The design is complete, and the front-end team builds from the prototype. It has not launched yet. The baseline audit found 45 spec items met, 37 partly met and 16 missing, and I fixed the flow against the spec, line by line. At the end of my design work, six people tested the prototype and scored it 81 out of 100 for ease of use (SUS), against a target of 70.
