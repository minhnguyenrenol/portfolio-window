THANK YOU FOR READING

I raise the bar by making it easier to meet.

  1. Agree the bar first

    Before screens, product, engineering and I agree what good looks like and how we’ll measure it.

  2. Write decisions down

    Every call gets a line: options, choice, reason, who asked. Arguments happen once.

  3. Teach on real work

    I coach in reviews of live work. That’s how I ran AI adoption for my NAB design chapter: one-to-one, on what people were designing that week.

How to read the numbers here.

Every figure on this site carries a stamp that says how I know it. If a number came from a test with five people, it says so. If it is someone’s estimate, it says whose.

Live
Measured in production
Tested
Measured in usability testing, with sample size
Modelled
Calculated from stated assumptions
Target
A goal set before launch, not a result
Estimate
Someone’s judgement, and I say whose
Record
A count of real artefacts
