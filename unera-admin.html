Five people scored it 81 out of 100 for ease of use. The team cut four features before anyone built them.

In-flight product · design complete, with engineering

  • Problem. Operators needed one console to fix stuck transactions, manage community centres and run the points rules. Because a tool that can change money can also hide a mistake, the client ruled out refunds and balance edits from the console.
  • What I did. Designed the overview as a queue of what needs an operator now, asked for a reason on every action with power over someone's money, and made reward rules append-only, so every past version stays on record. Wrote a rule, before the first module was built, that each module goes to engineering with its error and edge states. Before the main review, I told the team which parts AI had drafted, so they could cut what they didn't need.
  • Result. The team removed four features before anyone built them, and narrowed the first release to the Huma operators. The design is complete to spec v16, with 15 spec requirements and 11 review items traced to screens. At the end of my design work, five people tested the prototype and scored it 81 out of 100 for ease of use (SUS). It has not launched yet.
