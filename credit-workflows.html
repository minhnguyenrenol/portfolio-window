Live: the hand-off that took up to a day now takes about five minutes.

Live since mid-2026 · five minutes observed in real hand-offs

  • Problem. After NAB approves a corporate loan facility, often worth hundreds of millions, the banker has to ask operations to set up its limits. All five bankers we interviewed did that by email: details copied by hand from a spreadsheet and the credit platform, then hours to a day of corrections, with no way to see status.
  • What I did. Led the design for straight-through processing (STP). I set an ExQual target before any design, scored 136 interview notes in the open to pick three problems, and agreed a boundary with the designer leading a parallel redesign of credit submission, so the hand-off could ship first. Then I designed it as four steps inside the platform bankers already use, with eligibility checked first and every failure named.
  • Result. Live since mid-2026. When I watched bankers do real hand-offs after release, the whole hand-off took about five minutes, against up to a day before, and bankers confirmed it later. On the prototype, eight people rated it 4.40 out of 5 on ExQual, the bank’s experience score, in the Gold band against a Silver target. The work earned a Q3 FY26 Individual Award for ownership and stakeholder partnership.
