Live: the hand-off that took up to a day now takes about five minutes.

Live since mid-2026 · five minutes observed in real hand-offs

  • Problem. After NAB approves a corporate loan facility, often worth hundreds of millions, the banker has to ask operations to set up its limits. All five bankers we interviewed did that by email. They copied details by hand from a spreadsheet and the credit platform, then waited hours to a day while corrections went back and forth, with no way to see status.
  • What I did. I led the design for straight-through processing (STP) and coached Julie, a second designer. Before any design, I set a target for ExQual, the bank’s experience score. We scored 136 interview notes in the open to pick three problems, and I agreed a boundary with the designer leading a parallel redesign of credit submission, so the hand-off could ship first. The design has four steps inside the platform bankers already use, with eligibility checked first and every failure named.
  • Result. It has been live since mid-2026. When I watched bankers do real hand-offs after release, the whole hand-off took about five minutes, against up to a day before, and bankers confirmed it later. On the prototype, eight people rated it 4.40 out of 5 on ExQual, in the Gold band against a Silver target. The work earned a Q3 FY26 Individual Award for ownership and stakeholder partnership.
